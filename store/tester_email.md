**How to install:**

1. Open this link on your Android phone: [paste the Internal Testing invite link from Play Console here]
2. Tap "Become a tester"
3. Tap "Download on Google Play" — the app will install like any normal Play Store app

**What I'd love feedback on:**
- First impression — does the onboarding make sense?
- Any confusing screens or buttons?
- Anything that crashes or freezes?
- Does the timer work the way you'd expect?
- How does it look on your phone?
- What's missing that you wish was there?

Just reply to this email with whatever you find — even one-line thoughts are super helpful. Screenshots welcome.

Thank you 🙏 — this app exists because of friends like you willing to try it early.

## What to do with feedback

Track each piece of feedback in a simple list (notes app, Google Doc, or spreadsheet). Categorize as:

- **🐛 Bug** — something is broken
- **🎨 Polish** — small visual or wording improvement
- **✨ Feature** — new functionality requested
- **🤔 Confusion** — UX clarification needed

Prioritize fixes for issues mentioned by 2+ testers. Single-tester suggestions can be deferred or skipped.

After you've shipped fixes, send a "thanks, here's what changed" update to all testers. Keeps them engaged and likely to keep testing future updates.
