## Full description (max 4000 chars)
**Manicure Masterpiece** is a beautiful, focused routine planner and timer built for at-home nail technicians. Whether you're doing your own gel set or your sister's birthday nails, every step gets the time it deserves — no more burnt cure layers, no more guessing.

**Plan every set**
• Choose from 11 nail designs — solid, cat-eye, chrome powder, glitter, ombré, marble, polka dots, stripes, glazed donut, french tip, matte/gloss
• "All the same" or per-nail mode — give every nail its own design AND color
• Curated mood presets: Soft, Glam, Fierce, Minimal, Bold, Playful
• Daily nail horoscope to inspire your next set
• 10-color palette plus unlimited custom colors via a built-in hex picker
• Extract palettes from any photo — match an outfit or an inspo image

**Time every step**
• Guided countdown that auto-builds your routine based on your designs
• Haptic feedback at every transition (no need to watch the screen)
• 3-second countdown ticks before each step
• Screen stays awake during sessions
• Reorder, customize, or add your own steps
• One-handed mode for when your other hand is wet

**Save every set**
• Session history with photos, notes, and timestamps — your personal portfolio
• Look book to save inspo from anywhere
• Share beautiful, IG-ready cards directly to your favorite app
• Save and re-load any design as a preset
• Share presets with friends via QR code

**Built for the craft**
• Product library: keep track of every gel, polish, and chrome
• Barcode scanner to add products
• Virtual try-on: preview your design on a photo of your actual hand
• Light + dark spa-aesthetic themes
• Auto-saves everything as you go

**Privacy first**
Manicure Masterpiece runs entirely on your device. No account required. No analytics. No cloud sync. Your photos, presets, and history stay on your phone — you control them.

Made for nail enthusiasts who care about the details.
